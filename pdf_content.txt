Best dataset combination 
1. NASA Li-ion Battery Aging Dataset — primary dataset 
NASA's dataset contains battery charge/discharge cycles at different temperatures, with 
voltage, current, battery temperature, ambient temperature and capacity measurements. It 
was specifically collected for prognostics and Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction. 
NASA Li-ion Battery Aging Dataset 
2. NASA Randomized/Recommissioned Battery Dataset — additional validation 
This contains 26 battery packs tested under different constant and variable loading conditions 
and includes degradation, temperature, voltage, current and capacity information. 
NASA Randomized and Recommissioned Battery Dataset 
3. AMOVFLY UAV dataset — very interesting for your hackathon 
This dataset includes UAV flight information such as flight duration, power consumption, 
battery number, atmospheric pressure, air density, temperature, wind speed and weather. 
That makes it particularly useful for connecting UAV performance with environmental conditions. 
AMOVFLY Dataset

One important scientific point 
We should not simply claim that NASA's battery dataset is a Ladakh dataset. It isn't. It 
gives us battery behaviour under controlled temperatures and loads. Likewise, the UAV dataset 
gives environmental/flight relationships but isn't necessarily a Ladakh-specific dataset. 
So the scientifically defensible approach is: 
Existing datasets → learn degradation/performance relationships → build a high-altitude 
environmental model → simulate Ladakh operating conditions → predict 
risk/performance. 
That is much stronger than pretending the training data were collected in Ladakh.
