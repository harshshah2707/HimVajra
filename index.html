1. DRY AIR DENSITY (IDEAL GAS LAW)
ρ = P / (R_spec · T)

Calculated Density: 0.709 kg/m³ (vs 1.225 at SL).
2. CONVECTIVE HEAT TRANSFER COEFFICIENT
h_forced ∝ ρ^0.8 | h_natural ∝ ρ^0.5
Reynolds number drops proportionally to density.
Thermal Resistance Multiplier: 1.513×
θ_sa increases from 3.00 °C/W to 4.54 °C/W.
3. STEFAN-BOLTZMANN CHASSIS RADIATION
q_rad = ε · σ · A · (T_chassis⁴ - T_sky⁴)
ε = 0.88, A = 0.045 m², T_chassis = 335 K, T_sky = 233 K:
Radiated Dissipation: 20.9 W directly into cold sky.
4. PASCHEN'S LAW AIR BREAKDOWN
V_B = B·(P·d) / [ln(A·P·d) - ln(ln(1 + 1/γ))]
For 1.0mm gap at 54 kPa (P·d = 5.4 kPa·cm):
Breakdown Potential: 2,420 V (-41.7% from 4,150 V SL).
5. NORRIS-LANDZBERG SOLDER JOINT FATIGUE
N_f = C · (Δγ_p)^(-1.9) · f^(1/3) · exp(E_a / k_B·T)
Diurnal ΔT = 50°C yields N_f = 615 cycles (1.68 years).
HIMVAJRA Belleville spring standoffs yield 3,500 cycles (9.57 years).
6. ARRHENIUS ELECTROLYTE RESISTANCE
R_int(T) = R_0 · exp[(E_act/R) · (1/T - 1/T_0)]
